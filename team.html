The core team

An event about ideas still runs on people. These are the ones who make the arguing happen — from the first motion to the last vote of thanks.

( the one who started it all )

Ms. R. Placeholder

debate coordinator — gems founders school

For over a decade, the Debate Coordinator has built the speaking culture at GEMS Founders School — coaching speakers from their first nervous rebuttal to national finals, and insisting throughout that how you argue matters as much as whether you win. Legacy Debates exists in large part because of that insistence.

"I've watched shy students become speakers other people quote. That's the whole point of this event."
