Ms. R. Placeholder
Debate Coordinator, GEMS Founders School
“
I have watched shy students become speakers other people quote. That transformation is the entire point of this event.

For over a decade, the Debate Coordinator has built the speaking culture at GEMS Founders School — coaching speakers from their first nervous rebuttal to national finals, and insisting throughout that how you argue matters as much as whether you win.

Legacy Debates exists in large part because of that insistence. Under her guidance, the student team designs the motions, runs the rounds, and upholds the standard the event is named for.

Ms. R. Placeholder
