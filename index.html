About the event

A stage for ideas that outlast their speakers.

GFS Legacy Debates is the flagship debate event of GEMS Founders School, Dubai — a student-led forum where rigorous argument meets genuine respect for the other side. It brings together speakers from across the Emirates to contest the questions that deserve contesting.

More than a competition, Legacy Debates is an exercise in clarity, preparation and nerve. Speakers research, build, defend and concede — learning to listen as carefully as they speak, and to hold their ground without holding it blindly.

Whether you are stepping up to your first motion or returning as a seasoned finalist, the event offers the same thing: a serious stage, honest adjudication, and an audience that came to be persuaded.

The experience

What a round asks of you

01

Speak

Hold the floor. Build a case that stands on its own logic — clarity carries further than volume.

02

Challenge

Find the weakest beam in the strongest argument. Question the premise everyone else accepted.

03

Defend

Stand your ground under pressure. Concede the point that deserves conceding — and no other.

04

Leave a legacy

The round ends; the idea doesn't have to. Say something that stays in the room after you leave it.

From the President

The argument begins
before you arrive.

Aariz Placeholder
President, GFS Legacy Debates
“
Every speaker in this event will eventually sit down. What we care about is what remains standing when they do.

Legacy Debates was built on a simple conviction — that a school debate can be more than an exchange of rehearsed lines. This event is for the speaker who changes their mind mid-preparation, for the rebuttal that reframes an entire round, and for the audience member who leaves quietly rethinking something they were certain about that morning.

Whether you arrive as a competitor, an adjudicator or a guest, you will be part of an argument that started before us and will continue long after. We ask only one thing: come prepared to listen. Make your minutes count.

Aariz Placeholder
President, GFS Legacy Debates
