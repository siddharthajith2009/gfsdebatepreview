The Event

Built for ideas — and the courage to challenge them.

Legacy Debates is not a trophy hunt. It is a room full of people who take ideas seriously — who prepare, listen, concede when they must, and press harder when they shouldn't. Over one intense event, student speakers from across the region argue the questions that deserve arguing.

Hosted by GEMS Founders School, the event brings together motions worth losing sleep over, adjudication that rewards substance over theatre, and an audience that came to be persuaded. What is said here is meant to be carried out of the room.

A Word Before We Begin

Message from the President

Every speaker in this event will eventually sit down. The question we care about is what remains standing when they do.

Legacy Debates began with a simple conviction — that a school debate can be more than an exchange of rehearsed lines. We built this event for the speaker who changes their mind mid-preparation, for the rebuttal that reframes an entire round, and for the audience member who leaves quietly rethinking something they were certain about that morning. Whether you arrive as a competitor, an adjudicator or a guest, you will be part of an argument that started before us and will continue long after. Make your minutes count.

The Experience

What a round demands of you

Four disciplines, practised in every round — and remembered long after the final one.

01

Speak

Command the floor. Structure a case that stands on its own logic, delivered with clarity rather than volume.

02

Challenge

Find the weakest beam in the strongest argument. Question premises everyone else accepted without noticing.

03

Defend

Hold your ground under pressure without holding it blindly. Concede the point that deserves conceding — and no other.

04

Leave a Legacy

The round ends; the idea doesn't. Speak so that something of your argument stays in the room after you leave it.
