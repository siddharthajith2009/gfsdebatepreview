What GFS Legacy Debates is

Basically: a room where good ideas get taken apart, defended, and occasionally change someone's mind.

Legacy Debates is the student-run debate event of GEMS Founders School. No trophies-first culture, no rehearsed theatre — just motions worth losing sleep over, judges who reward substance, and an audience that actually came to be persuaded.

Message from the President

Every speaker eventually sits down. We care about what's still standing when they do.

We started Legacy Debates because school debates can be more than an exchange of rehearsed lines. This event is for the speaker who changes their mind mid-prep, the rebuttal that reframes a whole round, and the audience member who leaves quietly rethinking something. Come as a competitor, a judge or a guest — either way, you'll be part of an argument that outlasts all of us. Make your minutes count.

What a round asks of you

Four things, every round. The last one is the point.

/01

Speak

Hold the floor. Build a case that stands on its own logic — clarity beats volume, every time.

→
/02

Challenge

Find the weakest beam in the strongest argument. Question the premise everyone else skipped past.

→
/03

Defend

Hold your ground without holding it blindly. Concede the point that deserves it — and no other.

→
/04

Leave something behind

The round ends. The idea doesn't have to. Say something that's still in the room after you leave it.
